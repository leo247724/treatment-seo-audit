## Takeaways

- **Local intent** is owned by liveagaindetox.com (30 top-10 keywords, ~1,001 visits/mo); cumberlandheights.org scores 53/100 with 16 top-10 keywords.
- **Detox** is owned by liveagaindetox.com (21 top-10 keywords, ~754 visits/mo); cumberlandheights.org scores 5/100 with 1 top-10 keywords.
- cumberlandheights.org's authority is concentrated in recovery education: 12-step + mental-health topics carry 63% of its sampled traffic and 76% is informational vs a competitor median of 79%.
- Strongest topics: 12-step & recovery education (100), Benzos / barbiturates / sedatives (100), Local intent (53); weakest relative to the best competitor: Detox (5 vs liveagaindetox.com 100), Alcohol (5 vs recoveryunplugged.com 100), Mental health & dual diagnosis (22 vs recoveryranch.com 100).
- 5 gap keywords (2,750 searches/mo) rank top 20 for 2+ competitors but not for cumberlandheights.org; most fall in Local intent (5).
