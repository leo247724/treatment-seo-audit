Method: a brand counts as **mentioned** if any of its names appears in the answer text (case-insensitive; citation list excluded). "The Next Door" (Q2) counts for The Next Door Recovery. This is a single run on one engine. Answers change from run to run, so read this as a snapshot, not a ranking.

## Observations

- **Freeman Recovery Center leads the non-brand answers, mostly through its own pages.** Its domain was cited in 7 of the 8 non-brand queries: 27 of the 80 non-brand citation URLs, including insurance pages (`/insurance/bcbs/tennessee/...`), a "healthcare-workers" page and a "rehab-cost/affordable" page. It was named in the answer text in 6 of 8. Pages from APEX (5 queries), Tulip Hill (3) and Live Again Detox (2) were also cited. In some answers these pages were cited even though the brand's name never appeared: APEX in Q3/Q4, and Tulip Hill in Q4/Q7.
- **Cumberland Heights is absent from 6 of the 8 non-brand answers**: Q2 drug rehab, Q3 inpatient, Q4 medical detox, Q6 IOP, Q7 residential, and Q8 nonprofit. Its own domain was never cited for a non-brand query (0 of 80 URLs).
- **When Cumberland Heights does appear in a non-brand answer, it is mentioned first, but on the strength of directory pages.** In Q1 (best alcohol rehab) it is the first brand named, supported by alcohol.org, recovered.org and psychologytoday.com. In Q5 (Blue Cross Blue Shield) it is listed first, supported by startyourrecovery.org. Meanwhile Freeman's own BCBS pages were cited in that same answer.
- **The nonprofit query (Q8) names no tracked brand at all.** It recommends Hope of East Tennessee, CADAS, Memphis Recovery Centers, Grace House and CAAP, and cites addictioncenter.com, choosehelp.com, tn.gov and Psychology Today. Yet the Q9 answer describes Cumberland Heights as a "Nonprofit with a 177-acre campus near Nashville."
- **On its own brand queries, Cumberland Heights' site carries the facts while directories and reviews carry the verdict.** Q9 calls it "a strong fit" for residential, family and 12-Step care. For alternatives it compares Integrative Life Center, Mending Hearts and Samaritan (none of them tracked brands), all sourced to startyourrecovery.org. For Cumberland Heights' own Music Row IOP it cites recovery.com. Q10 calls it "legitimate, established ... with a strong overall reputation". It adds that reviews are mixed, with "concerns about mental-health care and fit for LGBTQ+ patients", and sources those reviews to startyourrecovery.org, rehabs.com and yelp.com.
- **Three tracked competitors never surfaced:** Recovery Unplugged, The Ranch/Recovery Ranch and Brentwood Springs Detox had zero mentions and zero citations across all 10 queries. Psychology Today was the most-cited third-party source, cited in 6 queries with 11 URLs.
